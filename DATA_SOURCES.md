# Data Sources

| Dataset | Description | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NYS Health Facility Registry | Licensed health facilities across New York State, including facility type, location, and certification dates | [health.data.ny.gov](https://health.data.ny.gov/resource/vn5v-hh5r) |
| U.S. Census Bureau — ACS 5-Year Estimates (2022) | Tract-level demographics: median age, median household income, total population | [census.gov/programs-surveys/acs](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs) |
| U.S. Census Bureau — TIGER/Line Shapefiles (2022) | Census tract boundary geometries for corridor counties | [census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html) |
| OpenStreetMap via Overpass API | Erie Canal centerline geometry | [overpass-api.de](https://overpass-api.de) · © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) (ODbL) |
| National Register of Historic Places | Historical industrial site records, designations, and descriptions | [nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/index.htm) |
| Rochester healthcare institutional histories | Verified founding years from hospital history pages, Wikipedia, NYS DOH records, and news archives | [Rochester Regional Health Archives](https://www.rochesterregional.org/about/history) · [URMC](https://www.urmc.rochester.edu) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_New_York) |
